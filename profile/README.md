## What it is

Merchants and creators sell with paywalls, promo codes and affiliate links,
and customers pay with mobile money. Every merchant is KYC-verified and
payments are tracked in real time.

## What it solves

Selling online in Madagascar usually means chasing mobile money transfers
by hand and trusting strangers. Ohayo Pay handles the checkout, the
tracking and the trust.

Ohayo Pay is invite-only for now: [request access](https://ohayopay.com).
