# 💸 Ohayo Pay – Payments Made Simple in Madagascar 🇲🇬

Ohayo Pay is a SaaS platform that helps merchants, digital creators, and local businesses in Madagascar sell products and services with **easy paywalls**, **affiliate links**, and **promo codes** — all backed by KYC and real-time payment tracking.

---

## ✨ What You Can Do

| For Merchants 🏪                 | For Affiliates 🤝                  | For Customers 👤                    |
|----------------------------------|------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Create static paywalls 💳       | Get unique affiliate links 🔗     | Pay easily via mobile money 📱      |
| Track payments in real-time 📡  | Track earnings and payouts 📊     | Get delivery at local landmarks 🗺️ |
| Offer promo codes 🎁            | Earn commissions 🪙               | Receive clear step-by-step status ✅ |
| Manage products & customers 🧾 | See link performance 📈           | Secure checkout with KYC-verified merchants 🔐 |

## 🧑‍💻 For Developers – Plug & Play SDK

Want to integrate payments into your own platform? Ohayo Pay provides an SDK so developers can:

- 🔌 Plug paywalls directly into their websites or apps
- 🧾 Trigger product delivery or webhook callbacks post-payment
- 📦 Use delivery-point suggestions via API
- 🎁 Generate promo codes and affiliate links programmatically
