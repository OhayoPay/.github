## What Ohayo Pay is

Ohayo Pay lets anyone in Madagascar sell online and get paid with mobile
money: create a paywall, share it, and track every payment as it comes in.

| For merchants | For affiliates | For customers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paywalls for products and services | Your own affiliate links | Pay with mobile money |
| Real-time payment tracking | Earnings and payouts | Delivery at local landmarks, no address needed |
| Promo codes | Commissions on every sale | Clear status at every step |
| Products and customers in one place | Link performance | Only KYC-verified merchants |

## What it solves

Selling online in Madagascar usually means checking mobile money transfers
by hand, sending screenshots as proof and trusting strangers. Ohayo Pay
handles the checkout, the tracking and the trust:

- **Mobile money first**, with no agent fees or hidden costs.
- **KYC for every merchant and affiliate** (national ID, selfie, SIM), to keep scams out.
- **Landmark-based delivery points**, made for how addresses work here.
- **A developer SDK** (coming soon) to plug paywalls, webhooks and promo codes into any site or app.

Ohayo Pay is invite-only while trusted merchants and affiliates are onboarded:
[request access](https://ohayopay.com).
